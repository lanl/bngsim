- **The steady-state stability certificate could call a stable root unstable, and report a wrong spectrum, where a near-zero eigenvalue's row fed a species nothing reads ([#1529](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/1529)).** A `$`-fixed source S feeding X at k = 1e7, X read by nothing: `steady_state(method="newton")` returned `root_stability == "unstable"` with `+6.9e-05` for an exact 0 and `-1.000069` for -1, where numpy and `steady_states()` give the exact spectrum; at k = 6.31e7 it reported -1 as -0.968. The dense eigensolver's balancing skipped such an isolated row without moving it, and the zero's condition number was k. It now isolates rows and columns as LAPACK's `dgebal` does, and reads their eigenvalues exactly. Over the corpus, every certificate's label is unchanged and every spectrum moves by under 1e-12 of its radius.
