- **`Model.describe()`: what a model is, in one call ([#1705](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/1705)).** `repr(model)` gave four counts, and what kind of quantity a species holds, its compartment, the reactions and which methods the model admits could not be read; a refused stochastic or network-free run was found by running it. `describe()` returns a JSON-serializable dict whose `str()` is a plain-text report: where the model came from (format, path, SHA-256), counts, parameters, compartments, species with their initial value, compartment and quantity (SBML and Antimony; absent for `.net` and BNGL), observables, functions, reactions, events, what reads the clock, where the next run starts, and for `ode`, `ssa`, `psa`, `nf`, `rm` and forward sensitivities, `ok` or the reasons each is refused, read from the checks the Simulator makes. Lists past `limit=` (default 50) are cut and say how many they left out.
