- **A sensitivity run that stalls short of a state crossing on the
  difference-quotient fallback is reported as what it is.** With
  `piecewise(1, A < q, 0)` beside a rate law the analytic sensitivity
  right-hand side declines (`max(A, 0.1)`, or anything reading `rateOf`), the
  run ended in `CVODE made no progress` 2e-5 to 2e-8 before the crossing: the
  difference quotient reads the jump there, and every step fails on it. That
  is now a `SensitivityUnsupportedError` that names the crossing and the rate
  law that was declined, in place of a solver failure.
