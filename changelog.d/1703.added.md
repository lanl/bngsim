- **A `Result` says what its numbers are and how its run went ([#1703](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/1703)).** `Result.record`, a JSON-serializable dict that `summary()` and `save()` carry, gives each column's quantity (concentration, amount or count), compartment, size and SBML units; the method, times, start state and parameters; the tolerances as the solver was given them; which code ran, including an analytical Jacobian retried on a difference quotient; the model file's hash; and the build. `Result.notices` lists every warning of the run, whatever the warning filters or logging level let through.
