- **`run_protocol` kept a derived parameter following what it reads after a `setParameter` set it to the number it already had ([#1738](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/1738)).** BNG2.pl's `setParameter` replaces the network's expression with the number, whatever the number. With `k2 k1*0.5` declared, `setParameter("k2",0.5)` then `setParameter("k1",3)` ran k2 = 1.5 where BNG2.pl runs 0.5: A(0.25) = 54.977 against BNG2.pl's 50.017. The same happened after a scan or a bifurcate of k2 that ended on that number, and in a scan of `k1` after `setParameter("k2",3)` at `k2 k1*3`. `set_param` overrides a derived parameter only while the value differs from its expression's (#188), so the replay now holds such a parameter at its number through every later write, and a `saveParameters` and `resetParameters` keep it so. A parameter held so reads nothing, so a later `setParameter("k1","k2*2")` is no longer refused as defined through itself. The hold is the replay's: on the model `run_protocol` returns, `set_param` follows its own rule (#188), and a k2 that holds the number its expression gives follows k1 again. The issue's two protocols now give BNG2.pl's numbers.
