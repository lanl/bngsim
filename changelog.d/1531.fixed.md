- **An expression's output sensitivity after `run()` reads `sum`, `avg`, `asinh`, `acosh`, `atanh` and other smooth built-ins ([#1531](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/1531)).** `output_sensitivities` raised for a function such as `sc*sum(Atot,K)` ("could not parse expression for differentiation") or `sc*asinh(Atot/K)` ("not representable in C"), while `sc*(Atot+K)` and `steady_state()` answered. These now return the derivative, as do `erf`, `erfc`, `log1p`, `expm1`, `mul`, `logn`, `ncdf`, `deg2rad` and `rad2deg`. `hypot`, `cot`, `sec`, `csc`, `sinc` and `tgamma` are still refused, by name.
