- **The SED-ML written from a BNGL protocol failed the official SED-ML L1V3 schema ([#1677](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/1677)).** `write_sedml_protocol`, and so `net_to_omex(bngl=...)`, `bngsim-omex pack --bngl` and `bngsim-net2sbml --sidecar`, wrote `listOfModels` before `listOfSimulations`; the lists are now in the schema's order. Experiments are numbered by position, so a protocol that holds one `Experiment` twice (`combine_protocols([p, p])`) no longer writes `sim_1` and `task_1` twice.
