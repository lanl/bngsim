- **Re-runs of models with events, time switches or state switches cost less ([#1669](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/1669)).** CVODE's root function evaluated every model function at every call, after every step and at every output row, though most triggers read the clock alone; it now evaluates only the observables and functions its expressions read. A state switch's residual, written out with every rule it reads, now computes each repeated subexpression and call once: BIOMD0000000385's ten residuals were 64,746 nodes of 183 different ones. A re-run of BIOMD0000000385 takes 19 ms where it took 117 ms (libRoadRunner: 4.8 ms), BIOMD0000000386 10 ms where it took 21 ms, and most of the other BioModels #1669 lists 10-30% less. Results are unchanged to the bit on the 1,271 rr_parity models that run, at two tolerances, and on the 1,113 that run with forward sensitivities; a shared residual can round a few units in the last place differently, which moved a run of a deep chain of rules under abs() by 5e-12 relative at rtol 1e-9.
