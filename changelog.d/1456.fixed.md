- **Forward sensitivities across a narrow pulse's corner were up to 1e-3 off on some output grids, with no error ([#1456](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/1456)).** CVODE finds the corner inside a step that went past it, and that step's corrector applies the post-corner rate to the whole step, so the sensitivities it hands over at the corner can be 1e-4 to 1e-2 off even though the step passed its error test. Those columns are now integrated again over that one step from its start; this is used only where the re-integration checks out as accurate. On the issue's triangle at rtol 1e-8 the worst grid moves from 1.4e-4 to 1.5e-5.
