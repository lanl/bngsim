- **A small jump at a state switch is read under a threshold species that
  moves fast.** A crossing was judged continuous where the switched flux
  changed by under 1e-6 of the rate that drives the crossing. With
  `if(Bobs < thr, kb, 0)` on a pool decaying from 1e8, that rate is 5e6, so a
  jump of 3 was dropped and every sensitivity column that moves the crossing
  time came back 0, with no warning: dY/dkdeg = 0 for 207.9. The tolerance is
  for a flux that vanishes on both branches, which differs across a pair of
  probes by its slope times the crossing's speed. Where both branches are
  extended to the root, as they are for a transversal crossing, that is out of
  the reading, and it is now held to the rounding of the flux's operands: what
  one ulp of each species the residual reads moves the flux by.
