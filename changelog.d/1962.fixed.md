- **`Result.check()`'s `"sensitivity"` check no longer passes a sensitivity that converges slowly as one held by a floor (issue #1962).** A series that moved at rtol/100 at least half as far as at rtol/10 was taken to be held by the solver's arithmetic floor, judged on its column's largest peak, and passed: on BIOMD0000000810 at rtol 1e-7, six series 0.3% to 2.6% off against rtol 1e-12 were passed. A series is now taken to be held only if its move from rtol/100 to rtol/1000 is still at least half as large; those six move 0.06 times as far there, and the check reports a diff. The run at rtol/1000 is made only where a series would otherwise be taken to be held.
