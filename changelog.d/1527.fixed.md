- **A `Sat` or `Hill` rate law on a repeated reactant now loads ([#1527](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/1527)).** BNG2.pl writes `A() + A() -> B() Sat(kcat,Km)` as `1 1,1 2 0.5*Sat kcat Km`, and `Model.from_bngl`, `Model.from_net` and `parse_net_file` refused it with "does not yet support repeated/non-unit reactant stoichiometry". The rewrite to a Functional law now reads reactant positions, as run_network does, so these models give run_network's trajectory (A(1) = 4.407330663 for Sat, 3.881617517 for Hill), in ODE, SSA and sensitivity runs. A second saturation constant on a repeated reactant (`0.5*Sat kcat Km K2`) is counted against the reactant positions, as BNG2.pl counts it.
