- **`method="rm"` runs a rate law that reads `time()`, and a table function indexed by time** (#1022, #862, #892). RuleMonkey treated a total propensity of 0 as final, so `if(time()>=6, k, 0)` never fired, and bngsim refused such a model. RuleMonkey 3.11.0 integrates a rate that reads the clock between events (richardposner/RuleMonkey#86), in steps of up to a thousandth of a call, which can step over a short window: a pulse of area 1 that is 0.1 wide fired nothing in a call of 1000. bngsim reads from the XML when the rates change with the clock and ends an engine call on each instant, so a pulse 0.00001 wide in a run of 100000 leaves 7402 of 20000 (closed form 7358). That takes a function that reads the clock smoothly, as a comparison with a number or a parameter, or through a table; any other (`floor(time()/5)`, a threshold that is an expression, a division by the clock) is refused with `ModelError`, as all were before. NFsim still refuses `time()`.
