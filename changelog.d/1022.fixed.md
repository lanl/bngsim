- **`method="rm"` runs a rate law that reads `time()`, and a table function indexed by time** (#1022, #862, #892). RuleMonkey treated a total propensity of 0 as final, so `if(time()>=6, k, 0)` never fired, and bngsim refused such a model. RuleMonkey 3.11.0 integrates a rate that reads the clock between events (richardposner/RuleMonkey#86). With 20000 `A(s~0)` and `k = 0.5`, `rm` now reads 20000 until t = 6 and 2691 at t = 10 (closed form 2707), and over `t_span=(5, 7)` it reads `[20000, 20000, 12148]` (closed form 12131). The #892 table fixture started from 100 `X(p~1)`, where nothing can fire until the table turns the rate on at t = 1, drains as the closed form says; it stayed at 100 on every row. NFsim still refuses `time()` and still reads a time-indexed table only when a reaction fires.
