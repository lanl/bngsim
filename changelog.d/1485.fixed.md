The dense solve now gives exactly 0 to the largest set of rows whose right-hand side is 0 and that read only each other, as KLU's block-triangular form does; every other component is SUNDIALS' dense LU's to the bit. A row with more than 16 entries off its diagonal, and any row reading one, is left to the LU. BIOMD0000000338 and 339 (#1304) now land on KLU's branch on dense at every rtol from 1e-6 to 1e-10.
