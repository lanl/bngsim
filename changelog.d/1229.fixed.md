- **A sensitivity run on a narrow continuous pulse was refused at tight tolerances, for a jump that does not exist** ([#1229](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/1229)). The #952 hold check ends a run whose state is held within a few ulp of a state-switch surface, because at a jump there is a saltation term to apply and no crossing time to take it from. It asked that of every surface, a bend included: where a triangle `max(0, 1 - abs(T-c)/(w/2))` returns to 0 the rate law is continuous and only its slope changes, which at rtol 1e-10 failed the error test as a jump would. The same pulse written with `if()` was refused the same way. A stalled surface is now read a step either side along the flow, and one the right-hand side is continuous across, or that no rate law reads, is carried over as a plain run's is, its columns unchanged. A stalled surface it jumps across is refused as before.
