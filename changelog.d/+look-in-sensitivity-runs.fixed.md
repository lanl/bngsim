- **A run with forward sensitivities lost a dose train's doses, and their sensitivities, with nothing said.** After a flat restart CVODE's steps grow until one takes a whole period at once and reads the rate law only where it ends. #998 and #1192 fixed that by reading inside such a step before it is taken, but only in runs without sensitivities. With sensitivities on, a late pulse over ten periods from t = 1,000 added nothing, a dose train that begins each period flat added 0.72 of its integral over 100 periods, and a noon dose with output rows every 8 hours added 0.02 over 100 days. dX/dk was lost by the same share. Such a run now reads inside the step too, and restarts where a step would fail, with its sensitivity columns carried across as at any other stop. While a column is in a comoving frame (#545) it does not, as a stop there would take the column out of its frame ahead of the crossing it was entered for.
