- **A comoving sensitivity case is found however the shift of its crossing is written** ([#550](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/550), [#760](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/760)). The shift `∂t*/∂p` of a crossing at `on/(w - v)` is `1/(w - v)`, which `cancel` can return as `-1/(v - w)`. Under that spelling sympy did not come back from the law's conditional, and the plan was dropped: an onset `on/(wb - wa)` had no comoving case in any process, and dX/dD of a window closing as a power was the plain column, 0.37% off at an exponent of 0.1 and `rtol=1e-8` (dX/d(on) 0.45%). For two names sympy's order of generators ties on, `w01` and `w1`, it was one spelling or the other by the hash seed: the same model with its cases under seeds 6 and 7 and without them under 0 to 5. Both spellings are tried now, and the one that leaves the least is taken. And a singular power is asked, through `cancel`, how its base moves along the shift, where sympy's own sum does not clear it: an onset `on/(kk*(wb - wa))`, `on/((wb - wa)*(wd - wc))` or `on/(wb - wa)^2` had dX/d(on) 0.45% off.
