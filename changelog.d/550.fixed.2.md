- **A comoving sensitivity case is found whichever way round the denominator of its shift is written** ([#550](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/550), [#760](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/760)). The shift `∂t*/∂p` of a crossing at `on/(w - v)` is `1/(w - v)`, and it removes the singular power from a column only as the law spells it. `cancel` returned `-1/(wa - wb)` for an onset `on/(wb - wa)`: the model had no comoving case, and dX/dD of a window closing as a power was the plain column, 0.37% low at an exponent of 0.1 and `rtol=1e-8`, in every process. For two names sympy's order of generators ties on, `w01` and `w1`, it was one spelling or the other by the hash seed: the same model with its case under seeds 6 and 7 and without it under 0 to 5. Both spellings are candidates now, and a shift has one case.
