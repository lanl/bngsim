- **A `.net` `MM` reaction with three or more reactants left all but two out of its rate ([#1502](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/1502)).** `S + E1 + E2 -> P + E1 + E2` at `MM kcat Km` ran with E1 as the whole enzyme, P(1) = 0.896350 where run_network gives 3.318492, and with E2 when the enzymes were listed the other way round, with no warning. Every engine read the first two reactants only. `Model.from_net`, `parse_net_file` and `ModelBuilder` now refuse an `MM` reaction with more than two reactants, as BNG2.pl refuses such a rule, and say how to write run_network's reading of it as a functional rate law. One or two reactants load as before.
