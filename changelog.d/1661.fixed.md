- **A species negative where the model has it so, but not declared negative, followed the wrong ODE with no warning once another species tipped a rate law non-finite ([#1661](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/1661)).** The non-finite retry (GH #135) read every concentration below 0 at 0, so a voltage written to -61 by `set_concentration`, or one declared at 10 that its own law takes below 0, was read at 0 beside a `sqrt` of an emptied species: V(40) = -328.6 for -69.8. The retry now reads at 0 only the slots the non-finite rows of f, or of the Jacobian, read, and the #1340 band reading likewise; of those, it puts back each slot further below 0 than any rounding of 0 (10^6 of its atol) wherever the reading stays finite without it. This holds in the time course and in the steady-state run test. Where no such reading is finite, the retry reads as before.
