- **`run_protocol`: after `resetParameters`, a parameter a `setParameter` set to an expression could follow the network's own expression instead, and a derived parameter that read it stayed a number ([#1876](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/1876)).** Writing back the parameter's saved number re-attaches it to the network's expression where the two agree before what that one reads is back. With `k3 k2*3` and `k4 k3*2`, `setParameter("k3","k1*9")` saved, then k2, k3 and k4 changed and reset, k4 stayed at 18 after `setParameter("k1",2)`, where BNG2.pl gives 36. The reset now has such a parameter follow its own expression again before it re-attaches the derived ones. A `setParameter` whose expression does not evaluate only while what it reads is on its way back (`sqrt(d)`, d briefly negative) is no longer refused, after a reset or otherwise.
