- **`net_to_omex` refused BNG2.pl's own network after an `addConcentration` before `generate_network` to a seed that a later `setParameter` changes ([#1740](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/1740)).** The check that a `.net` is the network its `.bngl` generates (#1683) read every seed at the parameters' final values. BNG2.pl's `addConcentration` reads the seed when it runs and makes the species a number, which a later `setParameter` does not move. So seed `B() B0` (B0 = 10), `addConcentration("B()",60)` and `setParameter("B0",20)` give B = 70 in BNG2.pl's `.net`, where the check wanted 80. The actions are now replayed in order, each with the parameters of its moment. A parameter a `setParameter` writes is read, before that step, as the `.bngl` declares it: a number, or an expression (`B0 10*k1`) that follows what it reads. An `addConcentration` after a `setConcentration` to an expression adds to the seed, as BNG2.pl does. A compartment volume a `setParameter` changes before generation does not stop the check. A seed, and a `setConcentration` to an expression, still follow their parameters to generation, as BNG2.pl keeps them. A `.net` a run rewrote is still refused.
