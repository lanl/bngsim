- **A smooth narrow bump of the clock in a rate law was stepped over** ([#1286](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/1286)). Examples are a Gaussian `exp(-((T - c)/s)^2)` or a sech² `1/cosh((T - c)/s)^2`. Away from itself such a rate is exactly 0 in doubles, so CVODE's error estimate was 0 and one step spanned the bump. X → Y at a Gaussian of area 0.5 gave Y = 0 for 2.212 at every tolerance, with dense output points and in sensitivity runs, with no warning. The run now stops at the centre of each bump in a clock: literal `time()`, or a counter fed at a constant rate. The step then ends on the peak, and the bump is integrated to the tolerance. Recognized: `exp` of a quadratic in the clock with a negative leading term, and `cosh`, `sech` or `tanh` of an affine function of it, which covers sech² spelled `1/cosh(u)^2` or `1 - tanh(u)^2`.
