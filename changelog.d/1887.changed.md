- **`Result.check()` judges every species, and reads a run's scatter at its tolerance ([#1887](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/1887)).** A species within 100 atols of zero, or not converged at the run's tolerance, was left out of every path check, so a rerun on which it departed from zero, or went non-finite, passed; each is now held to the run's whole scale and its own scatter. A path's allowance reads a species' drift as the larger of its move at a tenfold tighter tolerance and half its move at twice the tolerance: one tighter run understated a correct run's scatter where the run's own error was small by luck, and a correct path read as a disagreement. `check()` makes one more rerun for it. Where an event or a rule promotes a parameter into the state, each species now takes its own atol, matched by name, not the scalar ceiling. The developer-side differential harness, paths and steady states alike, judges by the same rule, species by species, where it judged the whole trajectory against the largest species' peak.
