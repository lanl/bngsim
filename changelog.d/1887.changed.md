- **`Result.check()` judges a species near zero on the run's whole scale, and takes each species' own atol where the run's state holds more than its species ([#1887](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/1887)).** A species within 100 times its atol of zero was left out of every path check, so one that sat at 0 in the run and departed in a rerun passed; it is now held to the allowance of a species at the run's largest peak and atol, which its solver noise stays within. Where an event or a rule promotes a parameter into the state, the per-species atol vector is longer than the species, and every species took the scalar ceiling; each is now matched by name. The developer-side differential harness judges paths by the same rule, species by species, where it judged the whole trajectory against the largest species' peak.
