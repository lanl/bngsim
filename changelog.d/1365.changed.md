- **The rows of `run_batch`, `run_replicates` and `parameter_scan` get seeds derived from the base seed, not `base + i`** ([#1365](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/1365)). Under `base + i`, an ensemble from `seed=1` was the one from `seed=0` shifted by a row. 49 of 50 trajectories were bit-identical, so ensembles from consecutive bases looked independent and were not. Each row's seed is now drawn from numpy's `SeedSequence(base)` and stamped on its `Result.seed` as before, so `run(seed=r.seed)` still repeats that row. Nearby bases give unrelated seeds, and the seeds of one call are distinct. **A seeded batch, ensemble or scan gives different trajectories than before**; `seed=None` behaves as it did.
