- **Compiled 4- and 5-argument `max` and `min` grouped their arguments unlike the interpreter** ([#1507](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/1507)). ExprTk pairs the first four arguments, `max(max(a,b),max(c,d))`, where the compiled call folded left to right, and with a NaN in third position the two give different values: `max(0,0,frac(),k)` with `frac()` = 0/0 gave X(10) = 10 compiled and 0 interpreted, both without a warning. The compiled call now groups as ExprTk does at every arity.
