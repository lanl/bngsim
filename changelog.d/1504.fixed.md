- **A time that is not finite no longer hangs a run** ([#1504](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/1504)). `Simulator.run(sample_times=[0, 5, inf])`, or a NaN last time, never returned on an ODE model that settles, and `timeout=` could not stop it: CVODE was handed a target it never reaches and spun inside a single CVode call, where the wall-clock budget is never checked. SSA and PSA ran until the budget or for ever, NFsim past it, and a NaN came back as a row labelled `t = nan`. `run()`, `run_batch()`, `run_replicates()`, `run_until()`, `parameter_scan()`, `bifurcate()` and `compute_all_sensitivities()` now raise `ValueError` at once for a time that is not finite, in the words the session validator and `set_time()` already used; `NfsimSession.simulate()` and `step_to()` refuse one as `RuleMonkeySession` does. The CVODE, SSA and NFsim backends refuse it at entry as well, for a caller of `_bngsim_core`.
