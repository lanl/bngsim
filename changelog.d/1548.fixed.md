- **A run whose state comes to rest on the threshold of a rate-law switch is refused by name, instead of returning whichever branch rounding picked (issue #1548).** Where a species relaxes exactly onto an `if()`/`piecewise` threshold, as `l-type-calcium-channel-dynamics.bngl`'s voltage does onto its recovery rate's, the run cannot tell which side of it the state is on, and the rate law read whichever side rounding left it: that model's Calcium() came back 957.96 on 301 outputs and 187.68 on 151, with nothing said. bngsim now raises a `SimulationError` naming the switch, the time and the jump, when the switch's residual has been off its surface, sits within the run's tolerance of it, no flow carries it onto the surface from either side, and the rate law jumps across it by more than the tolerance absorbs. Resolvable thresholds, continuous switches, slides, a species held at a cap, and one that never leaves its surface or that an event sets on it run as before. It is asked at each output, of BioNetGen networks in any run and of SBML models in sensitivity runs; a plain SBML run is not asked yet (issue #1588).
