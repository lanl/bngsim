- **A sensitivity time course on the difference-quotient fallback is refused for a model with a crossing on the state** ([#938](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/938)). One rate law the analytic sensitivity right-hand side declines (`max`, `floor`, `rateOf`) puts every column on CVODES' internal difference quotient, which reads the rate law at the state moved along each sensitivity. Just short of a threshold the state crosses, that is on the other branch, so a column took part of the jump before the crossing and all of it again at the crossing: `if(Aobs>thr,kb,0)` beside `kc*max(Aobs,0.5)` returned dY/dk = 14.52 for 10.2, with a warning. Such a model is now refused before the run: one with a rate-law condition or a step call that reads the state, a counter's threshold that a requested column moves, a step table function indexed by an observable, or `floor(time()/P)` with `P` requested. A condition on literal time and a steady-state solve still run.
