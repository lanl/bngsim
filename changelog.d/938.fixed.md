- **A sensitivity run on the difference-quotient fallback is refused at a state
  crossing where the rate law jumps.** One rate law the analytic sensitivity
  right-hand side declines (`max`, `floor`, `rateOf`) puts every column on
  CVODES' internal difference quotient, which reads the rate law at the state
  moved along each sensitivity. Just short of a threshold the state crosses,
  that is on the other branch, so a column took part of the jump before the
  crossing and all of it again at the crossing: `if(Aobs>thr,kb,0)` beside
  `kc*max(Aobs,0.5)` returned dY/dk = 14.52 for 10.2, with a warning that the
  columns were wrong. The run now stops at the crossing with an error. A step
  call on the state, `floor(Aobs)`, which nothing locates, is refused before
  the run. A crossing on literal time, and one where the rate law does not
  jump, are right on the fallback and still run.
