- **A result too large to allocate is a `MemoryError`, not a crash** ([#1524](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/1524)). `Result` sized its buffers and indexed its rows with 32-bit `int` products, which wrap past 2^31 values: 65536 species x 65537 output points came to a one-row buffer that every row then wrote past, and the process died with SIGSEGV after the heap had been overwritten; 49152 points wrapped negative and failed as `ValueError: vector`. Sizes and offsets are now computed in `size_t`, a request no buffer can hold is refused by name, and one that the machine cannot allocate fails as `MemoryError`. No ordinary model reached this: the wrap needs 16 GB of output.
