- **`Result.check()`: the run made again with one setting changed, and whether the answer moved (issue #1707).** Each check is a `rerun()` from the run's own start on a fresh model, so the session is untouched. By default an ODE run is checked at a tenth of its tolerances and at 8x the output points; `check("all")` adds a difference-quotient Jacobian, compiled against interpreted code, and libRoadRunner where it is installed. A stochastic run is made again at 10 other seeds, with each observable's mean and standard error. Each species is judged on its own scale, past the atol the run applied to it, so a small species is not hidden by a large one; a path may not differ by more than 1% of a species' peak for the tolerance alone. A disagreement is reported with what to do next, not raised; `budget=` bounds the wall time.
