- **`Result.check()`: the run made again with one setting changed, and whether the answer moved (issue #1707).** Each check is a `rerun()` from the run's own start on a fresh model, so the session is untouched. By default an ODE run is checked at a tenth of its tolerances and at 8x the output points; `check("all")` adds a difference-quotient Jacobian, compiled against interpreted code, and libRoadRunner where it is installed. A stochastic run is made again at 10 other seeds, with each observable's mean and standard error. Paths are judged by the developer-side differential harness's rule, which now imports it from `bngsim._check`, capped so a loose tolerance cannot forgive more than 1% of the peak. A disagreement is reported with what to do next, not raised; `budget=` bounds the wall time.
