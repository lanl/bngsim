- **`steady_state(sensitivity_params=...)` refuses `dY_ss/dp` at a state that is not the steady state a run reaches** ([#995](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/995)). `tol` bounds the residual `||f(y)||/n`, not the distance to the root, and a model whose concentrations are small passes it early: BIOMD0000000002 was accepted 0.02% from its steady state, and every column came back 5.9% from the derivative with nothing logged. The columns are solved a second time one Newton step on, and `SimulationError` is raised where one moves by more than 1% of its largest entry; solve again with a smaller `tol`. It is raised too where a run of `max_time` would leave more than 1% of a column unestablished, as it would for a species whose turnover is switched off at the steady state (12,500 in MODEL1607210000 where no run moves the species). The state `steady_state()` returns is unchanged. New on the result: `sens_root_column_shift`, `sens_root_relaxation`.
