- **`steady_state(sensitivity_params=...)` returns `dY_ss/dp` at the root, where the solver stopped short of it** ([#995](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/995)). `tol` bounds the residual `||f(y)||/n`, not the distance to the root: BIOMD0000000002 was accepted 0.02% from its steady state with every column 5.9% off, and a species at 1e-7 beside another at 1 where it starts, for dA*/dkd = -5.8e-9 against -2e-5. The columns are solved again one Newton step on, each species taken over its own concentration (`sens_species_scale`), and where one moves by more than 1% the state is stepped on until none does: `concentrations`, `residual` and the columns are then those of the stepped state, and `sens_root_newton_steps` counts the steps. `SimulationError` is raised where they have not settled in six, and where a run of `max_time` would leave more than 1% of a column unestablished (12,500 in MODEL1607210000 for a species no run moves). Without `sensitivity_params` the state is what it was. New on the result: `sens_root_column_shift`, `sens_root_newton_steps`, `sens_root_relaxation`, `sens_species_scale`.
