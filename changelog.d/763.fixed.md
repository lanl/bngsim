- **A large flux elsewhere in a model no longer hides a state switch's jump from
  forward sensitivities.** Whether a state-dependent rate-law switch jumps was
  decided by comparing the largest `|f⁻ − f⁺|` with the largest `|dx/dt|` over
  all species. One fast species the switch never touches, such as a 1e8-molecule
  pool turning over at 0.1/s, made a real jump of 3 read as roundoff. The
  saltation term was dropped, and every switch-time sensitivity column came back
  exactly 0 with no warning. The trajectory was unaffected. Each species is now
  judged against its own rate. A gap that grows with the probe distance counts
  as smooth, and a gap under the roundoff of the species' gross flux counts as
  continuous. The switch is continuous only where every species is.
