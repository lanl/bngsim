- **A large flux elsewhere in a model no longer hides a state switch's jump from
  forward sensitivities.** Whether a state-dependent rate-law switch jumps was
  decided by comparing the largest `|f⁻ − f⁺|` with the largest `|dx/dt|` over
  all species. One fast species the switch never touches, such as a
  1e8-molecule pool turning over at 0.1/s, made a real jump of 3 read as
  roundoff. So did the switched species' own large turnover. The saltation term
  was dropped, and every switch-time sensitivity column came back exactly 0
  with no warning. The trajectory was unaffected. The question is now asked of
  the flux of the reactions whose rate law reads the switch, species by species.
  A gap that grows with the probe distance counts as smooth, and one under the
  roundoff of that flux's gross size counts as continuous.
