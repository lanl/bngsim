- **A plain run pinned on a state switch whose residual is not exactly 0 on its
  surface is put across it (issue #963).** With the threshold species held on
  `thr`, `if(Aobs^2 > thr^2, …)` has a residual of +5.75e-17 while the
  condition reads false, and the #928 handler took that sign as "across and
  leaving". The run stayed pinned, returned with the switch not taken (Y = 0
  for 0.09), or ended in its timeout. A residual past zero by less than its own
  ulp is now read as on the surface, as `Aobs > thr` already was.
