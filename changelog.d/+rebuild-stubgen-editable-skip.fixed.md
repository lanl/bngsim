- **`scripts/rebuild_editable.py` failed in its stub step after a build that had succeeded.** The rebuild lists its build dir in `SKBUILD_EDITABLE_SKIP` and handed that environment to pybind11-stubgen. scikit-build-core's editable finder (since its #1612) stops resolving the package for a build dir listed there, so the generator could not import the extension it had just been built for: `ModuleNotFoundError: No module named 'bngsim._bngsim_core'`. The extension was installed; the stub was not regenerated and the version metadata was not refreshed. The stub step now runs without that entry.
