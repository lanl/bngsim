- **A fitted switch time on the instant of a condition it is composed with is refused** ([#951](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/951)). Two rate-law conditions on `time` that share an instant were read apart (#375) by taking each one's jump with the other already switched. Where the two are composed, as a product of two gates, the later of two times (`time >= tau && time >= t2`) or a window of no width, the jump with the other still to switch is a different one, and a parameter that moves one crossing alone has a kink there. The run returned one side of it: dY/dtau = −0.75 where the derivative is −0.75 from above and −0.375 from below, with no warning. Both jumps are read now, and the run ends with an error naming the switch where they differ. Conditions that add (two rate laws switching together, two gates summed in one) run as before.
