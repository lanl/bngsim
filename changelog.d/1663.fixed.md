- **`read_sedml` read a data generator as its first variable and dropped its math ([#1663](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/1663)).** A generator `A + B` came back as `species:A` (0.135 for 1), and `100*A` as `species:A`, with no warning. A generator is now an output only where its math is one variable as it stands; one that computes a value from its variables (a sum, a scaling, a normalization) is left out of `spec.outputs`, with a `ConversionWarning` that names it and its formula, since no output selector names it.
