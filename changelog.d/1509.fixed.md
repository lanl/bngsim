- **A stop condition that named something it could not read never fired, with no error** ([#1509](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/1509)). `add_stop_condition` accepted a misspelled observable, a parameter, a model function, a builtin such as `abs`, or BNGL's `^`. Each sample that reached it raised, the error was logged at DEBUG and skipped, and the run reported no condition met: `'A_totl < 10'` ran to t = 100 where `'A_tot < 10'` stops at t = 24, and `'A_tot < 5 or A_totl < 50'` stopped at t = 30 instead of 7. These are now refused with `ValueError` when the condition is added, and the message says what a condition can read. An expression that raises at a sample raises from the run, and an exception a callable raises propagates. A number now meets a condition where it is > 0, as documented; any nonzero value met it before. A tuple, and a species on a network-free run, are refused too.
