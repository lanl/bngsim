- **A `.net`/BNGL rate law switched on model state is no longer stepped over in
  a run without sensitivities (issue #897).** A window such as
  `if((time()>=4*S)&&(time()<4*S+w),k,0)` opens at a time the state decides, so
  no crossing stop can be placed at it, and a step could span the whole window:
  Y = 0 where the answer is 0.0540, at every tolerance. Each such condition in a
  rate law is now a CVODE root, as in a sensitivity run (#150) and in SBML
  (#194). No jump is applied, and the run does not restart at a root the flow
  opposes, which a trajectory parked on the threshold produces. SBML models are
  unchanged. On the 24 affected corpus models the restarts cost +44% steps.
