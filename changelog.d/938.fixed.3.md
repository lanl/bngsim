- **A condition on a requested parameter that sits near its threshold is refused on the difference-quotient fallback** ([#938](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/938)). `if(n > 1, kb, 0)` holds one way for a whole run, and is still a surface in `n`. CVODES' difference quotient reads the rate law at `n` moved by up to its size times the root of the relative tolerance, and with `n` that close to 1 one of the two readings is on the other branch: dY/dn came back 901 at n = 1 + 1e-6, 1.8e10 at n = 1 and 0.9 at n = 1.001, for 0. A model with no analytic sensitivity right-hand side is now refused where a requested parameter is within a quarter of itself of flipping such a comparison, where an equality on one holds, and for a step call, a step table or a sign written as a quotient on one. With the parameter not requested, or further off, it runs as before.
