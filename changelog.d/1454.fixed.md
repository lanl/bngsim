- **Forward sensitivities across a narrow pulse that jumps were slightly off, with no error ([#1454](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/1454)).** After a rate-law switch that jumps, the run restarts a hair past the switch, and the sensitivities were left behind the state there, as #1285 fixed for continuous switches. On a rectangle 0.003 wide in a clock, dY/dk_clock was 4.6e-5 off at every tolerance; it is now right to 3e-9 at rtol 1e-12. A re-taken step could also stop on the crossing it was re-taking and lose that crossing's jump in the sensitivities, which now restarts at the crossing instead.
