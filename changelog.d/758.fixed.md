- **Conservation laws across compartments of different size carry the sizes** ([#758](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/758)). A reaction between two compartments conserves a total of amounts, and the laws were row-reduced from the plain stoichiometry: `A` (size 1) `<->` `B` (size 2) reported `A + B`, which a run takes from 1 to 0.75, where `A + 2*B` stays at 1. `steady_state(method="newton")` held the wrong total and reported the eigenvalue -1.5 for -2, and `steady_state(sensitivity_params=...)` was 33% to 90% off (refused since #704). The laws are found for the amounts now, follow a compartment size written with `set_param` (per model, so each `steady_state_batch` entry holds its own), and leave out a species whose compartment a rate rule or an event resizes. The steady-state sensitivities of such a model are computed again where the reduced Jacobian is well conditioned (`sens_jacobian_rcond` of 1e-8 or more), and stay refused below that. Before a solve, `steady_state` asks the right-hand side whether it keeps every reported total, and raises `SimulationError` where it does not.
