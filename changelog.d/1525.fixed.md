- **`method="nf"` deleted a whole complex once per matching molecule, not once per complex** ([#1525](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/1525)). A rule that deletes a whole species without `DeleteMolecules` (`A(d!1).A(d!1) -> 0 k`, or `M() -> 0 k` on a complex holding two M) fired once for every match the complex held, where BNG2.pl's network gives each complex one reaction at `k`. A homodimer pool left 735.8 complexes at t = 500 where 1213.1 is the mean, a 4-ring's ran at 4k, and a local-function rate 2× too high, with no error; `NfsimSession` went the same way. NFsim now fires such a pick with probability 1/m, m the matches in the picked complex, with complex bookkeeping on or off. `{MatchOnce}`, whose per-complex count never took effect in NFsim, is counted the same way: a MatchOnce state change on a homodimer ran at 2k. A Michaelis-Menten rule with such a reactant whose complex may hold two matches is refused, since its law is not linear in the count. `method="nf_exact"` was right throughout.
