- **`method="nf"` deleted a whole complex once per matching molecule, not once per complex** ([#1525](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/1525)). A rule that deletes a whole species without `DeleteMolecules` (`A(d!1).A(d!1) -> 0 k`, or `M() -> 0 k` on a complex holding two M) fired once per match, where BNG2.pl's network gives each complex one reaction at `k`. A homodimer pool left 745.7 complexes at t = 500 where 1213.1 is the mean, a 4-ring's ran at 4k, with no error; `NfsimSession` too. NFsim now fires such a pick with probability 1/m, m the matches in the picked complex. `{MatchOnce}`, whose per-complex count never took effect in NFsim, is counted the same way: a MatchOnce state change on a homodimer ran at 2k. Where the complex may hold two matches, `method="nf"` refuses such a rule under a Michaelis-Menten law or a local function read on one molecule. RuleMonkey counted the deletions right but ignores `MatchOnce`, so `method="rm"` now refuses it where the rule acts on that reactant. Still open: `MatchOnce` on a pattern that matches one molecule twice through a repeated component (`S(b~0)` on `S(b~0~1,b~0~1)`) runs at 2k in both engines.
