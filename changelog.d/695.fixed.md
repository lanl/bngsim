- **A parameter that only sets a stoichiometry or a conversion factor at load is refused where it is written** ([#695](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/695)). An SBML `conversionFactor`, an L2 `<stoichiometryMath>` over parameters, an `initialAssignment` onto a `speciesReference` id and the id itself are folded into the reaction's coefficients when the model is built. The source parameter stayed writable and nothing read it: `set_param` was a silent no-op in ODE and SSA runs (with a stoichiometry of `2*f`, P(1) = 12.64 where a rebuild at `f = 3` gives 37.93; BIOMD0000000040's `f`), and its sensitivity column was an exact 0. Both are refused now, as for a parameter folded into a compartment's size (#696), and so is a parameter that an `initialAssignment` bngsim could not keep symbolic reads, which was named in a warning at load and nowhere else. 15 of 1,297 BioModels that load have such a parameter.
