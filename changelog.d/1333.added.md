- **`solver_stats` counts what a difference-quotient Jacobian costs** ([#1333](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/1333)). `n_rhs_evals` is CVODE's count of the integration's own right-hand-side evaluations, so the ones spent forming a Jacobian by difference quotients, including #1302's jump test, showed in no counter. They are now `n_jac_rhs_evals`, for bngsim's dense and colored quotients and CVODE's own. `n_jac_formations` is the number of Jacobians actually formed, beside `n_jac_evals`, which counts the linear solver's setups and can reuse one. On `jacobian="fd"` runs of the ODE perf suite they come to 0.5-12x `n_rhs_evals`, about two per species per Jacobian formed. The nightly's work counters carry and compare `n_jac_rhs_evals`, from the next re-recorded baseline on.
