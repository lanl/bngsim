- **A sensitivity run on a `clamp()` pulse over the state ran on the difference quotient, its clock-rate column up to five-fold off and not converging** ([#1227](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/1227)). Since #1131 a pulse of `min`, `max` or `abs` has an analytic sensitivity right-hand side; `clamp` had no sympy counterpart and was declined. It is now a bend twin with ExprTk's ordered semantics, so `clamp(0, (Tobs-c+w/2)/(w/2), 1)` beside its shifted copy gives dY/dk_clock to 3e-5 at rtol 1e-9 where the difference quotient gave it to 7%. Only a clamp whose bounds are numbers with lo <= hi is a bend: crossed bounds make it a step, which still declines, and so do bounds over parameters or state. The analytic state Jacobian differentiates a clamp too, as it does `min` and `max`.
