- **Forward sensitivities across a narrow pulse whose rate law reads `time()` were off by up to 3e-4, with no error ([#1455](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/1455)).** The restart a hair past a rate-law switch moves the state and keeps the clock, and where the right-hand side reads the clock, where it restarts depends on when the switch crossed, which moves with each parameter. The sensitivities now carry that term. A triangle 0.003 wide in `Tobs + time()` is now right to the clock's own rounding, 6e-6 at t = 25. Models that do not read the clock are unchanged.
