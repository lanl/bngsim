- **A forward-sensitivity run of a switch the clock carries across against the state stalled, or came back wrong ([#1776](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/1776)).** A narrow pulse in `3*time() - Tobs`, with `Tobs` rising at 2 per unit time, failed with "CVODE made no progress" at its near foot at every tolerance. The same crossing written as a jump, `if(3*time() - Tobs > c, kx, 0)`, ran until the timeout, and with `0.1*Tobs` in place of `Tobs` it returned its clock-rate column 100% off with no error. The plain run was right. The restart past such a crossing now moves the species the flow moves onto the far side, at most 16 times what the restart already moves them. The stall carry (#1229) takes such a crossing too, and the clock-rate column reads the moved restart without counting the clock's share twice. These pulses now come as close as the same pulse with the clock behind the state: within 6e-6 of the closed form at rtol 1e-11 on the grids tested. A crossing whose restart already lands past it is unchanged, and so is one the clock outruns its species on by more than that bound; issue #1779 lists what is left there.
