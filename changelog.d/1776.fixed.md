- **A sensitivity run of a switch the clock carries across against the state stalled or came back wrong ([#1776](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/1776)).** A narrow pulse in `3*time() - Tobs`, with `Tobs` rising at 2 per unit time, failed with "CVODE made no progress" at every tolerance. The same crossing as a jump, `if(3*time() - Tobs > c, kx, 0)`, ran to the timeout, and with `0.1*Tobs` it returned its clock-rate column 100% off with no error. Plain runs were right. The restart past such a crossing now moves the species onto the far side, each by a flow-weighted share and at most 16 times what the restart already moves it; the stall carry (#1229) takes such crossings too; and the clock-rate column no longer counts the clock's share twice. These pulses are now within 6e-6 of the closed form at rtol 1e-11. Plain runs are unchanged. Where the clock outruns the species beyond that bound, as in `3*time() - 0.05*Tobs`, runs still stall or are wrong; see issue #1779.
