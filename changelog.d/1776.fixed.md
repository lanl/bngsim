- **A forward-sensitivity run of a switch the clock carries across against the state stalled at every tolerance ([#1776](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/1776)).** A narrow pulse in `3*time() - Tobs`, with `Tobs` rising at 2 per unit time, failed with "CVODE made no progress" at its near foot, and the same crossing written as a jump, `if(3*time() - Tobs > c, kx, 0)`, ran until the timeout. The plain run was right. The restart past a crossing keeps the time and moves the state along its flow, which here put the state back on the side it came from. The stall carry (#1229) only carried a crossing the species themselves carry. The restart now moves the state onto the far side where the clock carries the crossing, the stall carry takes such a crossing too, and the clock-rate column reads the moved restart without counting the clock's share twice. Such a pulse is now as accurate as the same pulse with the clock behind the state: within 6e-6 of the closed form at rtol 1e-11 on the grids tested. Crossings the species carry are unchanged.
