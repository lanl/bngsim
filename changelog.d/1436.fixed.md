- **SSA runs with recurring events far from t = 0 came back slow, with no error ([#1436](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/1436)).** On the continuous loop (a rate that reads the clock, or a rate rule), each event's restart counted the hazard to the event's time and moved the clock to the double it rounds to, up to half a spacing of the doubles later; and a firing whose rounded time reached the double where an event's trigger turns over drained that event and restarted its waiting time from the rounded time. 500 events a unit busy at t = 1e12, where the doubles are 1.2e-4 apart, fired 2.27% too slowly (z = -17.6). The hazard now runs to the double the clock moves to, what passes E there goes on to the next waiting time, and a firing's restart carries the time its rounding moved the clock past it: that case comes back within about 0.05% of the exact rate, and 1000 events a unit at a rate of 500 within noise. Near t = 0 these agree with what ran before to rounding.
