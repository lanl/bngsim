- **A run pinned on a state-switch surface gets across it.** A run without
  sensitivities restarts at a state-switch root only where the solver's own
  trajectory resolves the crossing (#897). With one surface spelled twice,
  `if(Aobs < thr, …)` and `if(Bobs > thrB, …)` for a conserved `A + B`, and a
  slow crossing, the roots were found in steps the discontinuity had already
  collapsed and none was resolved. The run stepped on with `A` exactly on
  `thr`: a step long enough to move it by an ulp failed the error test on the
  jump, and a shorter one left it where it was, so CVODE crept 1e-4 of time
  per 500 steps until the wall clock ended the run. Where a whole batch of
  steps has been spent that way, the state is now carried across the surface
  along the flow and the integrator restarts there. A slide along a surface,
  where both branches point into it, is left as it was.
