- **A rate-law switch whose window opened and closed inside one solver step was skipped, with nothing said.** After a stop or a restart where nothing changes, CVODE's second step may be 10,000 times its first, and CVODE finds a switch's crossings only where its two ends are on different sides. In a run with a time stop, the look of issue #998 read that step's rate at three places inside it. A window whose effect was already spent at those places, such as the tail of a pulse it lets through, went unseen: S drifting up past a threshold that swept up on time or on a clock species, Y gained 0 where the pulse gives it 6.7e-5, at every tolerance down to rtol 1e-6. The look now also reads each switch's side at those places. Where a switch is on the other side from both of the step's ends, the step is cut there, and CVODE's own root search finds the crossing.
