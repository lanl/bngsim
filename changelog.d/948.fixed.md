- **A sensitivity to a counter clock's own initial value or rate is refused under a window that opens or closes as a singular power of the counter** ([#948](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/948)). The frames that carry a column across such an edge are of the parameters the power is written in, `on` and `D`. A column that moves the counter species itself, its seed parameter, its rate constant or its initial-condition axis, moves the edge too and has no frame: dX/dT0 came back -0.6817 for -0.6843 under a closing power, and the run ended in `CVODE made no progress` under an opening one. Such a run is now refused where it starts, with `compute_all_sensitivities()` and its default columns included, which ask for the counter's rate constant. That is asked of the powers themselves, so a window written in numbers, `(t-4)/4`, is refused as well. An exponent counts between 0 and 1 at the run's values, and under 0 where the power's base is 0 at the rate law's own condition, a pole on the window's edge. With any other, a window that opens and closes as a step, or a second counter that no such power reads, the counter's own column is not refused.
