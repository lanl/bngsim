- **A sensitivity to a counter clock's own initial value or rate is refused under a window that opens or closes as a singular power of the counter** ([#948](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/948)). The frames that carry a column across such an edge are of the parameters the power is written in, `on` and `D`. A column that moves the counter species itself, its seed parameter, its rate constant or its initial-condition axis, moves the edge too and has no frame: dX/dT0 came back -0.6817 for -0.6843 under a closing power, and the run ended in `CVODE made no progress` under an opening one. Such a run is now refused where the counter crosses a rate-law threshold. With an exponent that is not singular at the run's values, or a window that opens and closes as a step, the counter's own column runs as before.
