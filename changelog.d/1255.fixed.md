- **Building a Simulator took exponential time where a rate law reads a diamond of rules** ([#1255](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/1255)). With `r_i := sin(r_{i-1}) + cos(r_{i-2})` under a compartment size, the dilution term's rate law written out with its functions doubles with each pair of rules and does not collect, so the analytical Jacobian derived, checked and printed a derivative as long: 3.1 s at 12 rules, 57 s at 18 and 110 s at 22, against under 10 ms with `jacobian="fd"`. A rate law past the inlining limit (20,000 characters written out) that does not collect shorter now declines the analytical Jacobian from its predicted length, before the text is written (`analytical_jacobian_decline["kind"] == "written_out"`), and the model runs on the finite-difference Jacobian: 0.02-0.03 s at any depth. Across the corpus, five BioModels that spent their whole 20 s derivation budget before declining now decline at once, with identical results; BIOMD0000000385 and BIOMD0000000628 move to the finite-difference Jacobian with builds of 9.6 s and 18.1 s becoming 0.3 s and 0.05 s, and results within the integration error.
