- **A kinetic law's local parameter named like a compartment, or like a species outside the reaction, was read as that global in a mass-action law ([#1503](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/1503)).** In `C*k*S` with a local `C = 7`, the local was cancelled against the storage volume as the compartment, and in `k*S*Q` with a local `Q` it was multiplied in as the species `Q`: [S](2) = 2.456 and 2.222 where SBML scoping gives 0.740. The right-hand side, the Jacobian, the SSA propensity, both codegen paths and forward sensitivities shared the wrong rate, with no warning. The mass-action classifier now reads a law's locals first, as the Functional path already did, so the reversible splitter and a local shadowing the reactant itself (L3V1) read them too. A shadowing local also no longer marks the reaction as reading the compartment's size, which refused a write to that size for nothing.
