- **A parameter set to an expression of other parameters was evaluated once, where BNG2.pl keeps it as that expression ([#1682](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/1682)).** After `setParameter("k2","k1*3")` and `setParameter("k1",5)`, BNG2.pl runs k2 = 15 (A(1) = 75.00); `run_bngl`, `run_protocol` and the SED-ML export ran k2 = 3 (37.52), and a scan over k1 gave 50.12 for 75.00. The replay now keeps such a parameter as its expression through later actions, scans and `saveParameters`/`resetParameters`, until a number is set, and refuses one defined through itself, or through the network's own expressions, as BNG2.pl does. The SED-ML sets it again after each change to what it reads, a reset included, and at each point of a scan of it; a change to a parameter the SBML computes by an `initialAssignment` removes that assignment first, which overrode it. A value read from another tool's SED-ML is still evaluated once, as SED-ML says (`StateChange.evaluated_once`).
