- **`copy.copy()` of an `NfsimSession` or a `RuleMonkeySession` shared the original's engine, and dropping the copy destroyed it.** The original's next `simulate()` failed with "No active session". Copying, `copy.deepcopy()` and pickling a session are now refused with a `TypeError` that says to build a second session from the XML, as for `Model` and `Simulator`. Also, a session whose constructor failed (an unknown keyword such as `seed=`, which belongs to `initialize()`, or a build without the engine) no longer prints a spurious `AttributeError` from `__del__` beside the real error.
