- **A window spelled as one comparison of `abs()` or a square is no longer
  stepped over (issue #904).** `if(abs(A-5) < 0.01, k, 0)` and
  `if((A-5)^2 < 0.0001, k, 0)` have a residual that falls to zero and comes back
  within one step, so a root on it saw one sign at both ends and the window was
  lost: Y = 0 for 0.1 at every tolerance, and with sensitivities the square gave
  dY/dk = 0. Such a comparison is now rooted, and jumped, at each of its two
  edges.
