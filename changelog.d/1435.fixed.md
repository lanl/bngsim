- **Forward-sensitivity refusals that can turn on which parameters are requested came back as a plain `SimulationError`**, told from a solver crash only by their text (issues #1435, #1560). These are the solver's refusals partway through a run where a requested parameter moves a crossing or a fire, moves two times apart, or moves the run onto an edge. They now raise `SensitivityRefusedForParametersError`, a `SensitivityRefusedDuringRunError`, so they are a `SensitivityUnsupportedError` and still a `SimulationError`. `compute_all_sensitivities` still retries such a chunk one column at a time, and reports the columns refused alone by the same type, without the tolerance advice meant for a solver failure. A switch crossing after more event fires than are kept is refused whatever is asked, and raises `SensitivityRefusedDuringRunError` at once. A `run_batch` row stopped by a run-time refusal is named again (`run_batch row N: …`). A fire the solver found but did not locate, which asks to be reported, stays untyped.
