- **On Apple Silicon and other arm64 hosts, a MIR-compiled function with more than a megabyte of code could jump to the wrong place.** MIR's aarch64 backend masked each branch offset to its instruction's field, so a conditional branch over more than 1 MiB of code (`b.cond`, `cbz`, `cbnz`) went somewhere else. BIOMD0000000471's forward-sensitivity run under `BNGSIM_CODEGEN_JIT=mir` died with a bus error. An `if` over 100,000 statements returned 4999.2 instead of 1 without an error. A local carry in `third_party/mir/mir-gen-aarch64.c` now gives such a branch the opposite condition and a jump over an unconditional `b`. A label that is still out of reach is refused by name with a `RuntimeError`: an `adr` to an address-taken label, or a `b` past 128 MiB. Functions under 1 MiB of code, and x86-64, are unchanged.
