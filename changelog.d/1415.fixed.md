- **A bracket after a function call dropped everything before it, with no error** ([#1415](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/1415)). ExprTk multiplies a variable or a bracketed group against a bracket after it, but after a call the bracket opened a second statement, and the expression's value was that statement's: `k*exp(-A)(B)` with A = 3, B = 5 gave 5 for 0.124, and a `.net` rate law written so ran as its last factor. Inside a bracket it was refused with ExprTk's bare `ERR244`. Such an expression is now refused at load, naming the call (`exp(-A)`), as BNG2.pl rejects it as a syntax error. `A(B)`, `(A)(B)` and `2(B)` are read as products, as before; no model in the 1,323-model BioModels corpus or the repository's 177 `.net` files is written this way.
