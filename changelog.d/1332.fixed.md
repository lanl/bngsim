- **A forward sensitivity to a parameter sitting on a kink of a rate law returned one side's slope, or neither** ([#1332](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/1332)). `0 -> X` at `10*abs(q) + s` with q = 0 has slopes of −47.62 and +47.62 in q at its steady state, and no derivative. bngsim returned dX*/dq = 0. `10*min(q, 1) + s` at q = 1 (slopes 47.62 and 0) returned 23.81. Both happened at a steady state and in a run, with no error. Such a column is now refused with `SensitivityUnsupportedError`, naming the parameter, the reaction and the bend (`abs`, `min`, `max`, `clamp`, `if()`). The check covers bends over parameters alone, at the values the run, steady state or batch row has, so a `set_param` onto a kink after the `Simulator` is built is refused too. A parameter the bend does not move, and a tie of arguments with one slope, are not refused.
