- **What a model is, asked by another tool's name, now names `describe()` ([#1843](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/1843)).** `model.summary()`, `.info()`, `.to_dict()`, `.compartments`, `.reactions`, `.units` and their neighbours raised a bare `AttributeError`, as did libRoadRunner's `getInfo`, `getCompartmentIds`, `getReactionIds` and `getNumReactions` on a `Model` or a `Simulator`. Each now names the call to make: `model.describe()`, the part of it that holds the answer, or `model.n_reactions`, with what it counts. The agent guide's translation table lists them.
