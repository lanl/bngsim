- **A scalar `atol` you pass is now read as a ceiling, per species, like the
  default.** To hold every species to exactly the number given, pass
  `atol=bngsim.ExactAtol(value)` (or read a SED-ML file with
  `read_sedml(..., exact_atol=True)`). Each species takes
  `min(atol, rtol * max(|x_i(0)|, floor))`, so no species is held looser than
  the number, and a species far below it is still resolved. This covers an
  `atol` given to `run`, `run_until`, `run_batch`, `parameter_scan`,
  `compute_all_sensitivities` or `set_tolerances`, and a SED-ML file's
  `KISAO:0000211`. Held exactly, a SED-ML file's `atol=1e-9` at `rtol=1e-6` left
  BIOMD0000000457's species_42, near 1.6e-7, 13% off. At the SED-ML files' own
  tolerances, runs of 1,261 BioModels off their reference by over 1% go from 56
  to 23. None got worse past 1%, none newly fail, and the corpus takes 4.5%
  longer. At the RoadRunner parity suite's rtol 1e-9 and atol 1e-12 they go from
  16 to 5, in 10% fewer steps. A vector is still used exactly as given, and
  `steady_state(atol=...)` still takes a scalar as given.
