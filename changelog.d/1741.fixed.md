- **`net_to_sbml` and `net_to_omex` refused a derived parameter that uses `_pi()` or `_e()`, and wrote a parameter named like a MathML constant as that constant ([#1741](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/1741)).** BNG2.pl writes `k2 _pi()*k1` into the `.net` as declared; the call's `()` was left on pi's placeholder, which then read as an unknown function, and `_e` was not translated at all. Both are now `<pi/>` and `<exponentiale/>`, bare or called, in the SBML and in a SED-ML action's value (`setParameter("kr","_pi()*k1")`, which was left out as lossy). The same translation read a parameter, observable or function named `exponentiale`, `avogadro`, `true`, `false`, `inf`, `infinity`, `nan` or `notanumber` (any case) as that constant wherever an expression referred to it, with no warning: the tracked `tests/data/exprtk_reserved_words.net` (issue #18), whose parameters `true` and `false` are 0, converted with its rate at 2 instead of 1. Such a symbol is now written as a reference to itself, as `pi` already was.
