- **`net_to_sbml` and `net_to_omex` refused a derived parameter that uses `_pi()` or `_e()`, and wrote a symbol named like a MathML constant, or like `time`, as that constant ([#1741](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/1741)).** BNG2.pl writes `k2 _pi()*k1` into the `.net`; the call's `()` was left on pi's placeholder, and `_e` was not translated. Both are now `<pi/>` and `<exponentiale/>`, bare or called, in the SBML and in a SED-ML action's value. `write_bngl` had the same fault (`3.141592653589793()*k1`, and a bare `_e`, which BNG2.pl refuses) and now writes each as its number. A parameter, observable or function named `exponentiale`, `avogadro`, `true`, `false`, `inf`, `infinity`, `nan`, `notanumber` or `time` (any case) was read as that constant, or as the clock, wherever an expression referred to it, with no warning: the tracked `tests/data/exprtk_reserved_words.net` (issue #18), whose parameters `true` and `false` are 0, converted with its rate at 2 instead of 1. Such a symbol is now written as a reference to itself, as `pi` already was; the call `time()` is still the clock. The refusal of `_pi(2)` now names `_pi`, not an internal placeholder.
