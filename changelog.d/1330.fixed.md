- **Where there is no closed-form Jacobian, steady-state sensitivities and `Model.jacobian()` are exact now, not difference quotients** ([#1330](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/1330)). With `jacobian="fd"`, or one rate law the closed form declines, a law could be written whose secants agreed at every step the checks took: dG\*/ds came back 2.13 and 4.76 for 0, and dX\*/dp 2.02e-4 for 3.62e-4, with no error. The Jacobian, ∂f/∂p and a function's row of `output_sensitivities` are now taken by dual numbers through the interpreter, each law differentiated along the branch it takes. Many #1002 refusals are lifted, including function rows the compiled chain rule declines. At a state exactly where a law changes branch, both sides are taken and the answer is refused where they differ. The sources read `"automatic-differentiation"`. Models with `rateOf` or a ring of functions keep the checked difference quotients.
