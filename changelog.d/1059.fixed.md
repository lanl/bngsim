- **An SSA run from a large `t_start` no longer loses its waiting times to
  rounding (issue #1059).** Each firing was placed at `t + tau` in doubles, so
  once the total propensity times |t| neared 1e15 a waiting time below the
  spacing of the doubles near t rounded away: a decay from 1e5 copies over two
  seconds from `t_start = 1e12` fired 1.6 times too many events (z = -287, no
  error). The clock is now kept as an origin plus the elapsed time, so waiting
  times add at the scale of the run's span. Runs from `t_start = 0` are
  unchanged to the bit. A model whose rates read the clock still integrates its
  hazard on absolute time.
