- **A rate-law switch's window that opened and closed inside a step the look does not read was skipped, with nothing said.** CVODE's root search reads a switch's side only at a step's two ends. The look (issue #998) reads inside a step only where the step takes a quarter or more of what is left, and then at three places. A window narrower than that, between its places or in a shorter step, was missed. A threshold that rose past S faster than S rose past its ceiling opened a window 0.047 wide in a run of three steps, and Y gained 0 where the window gives it 0.4676, at every tolerance. Now each step a watched run takes is held against what the root search read at its ends. A residual that came closer to its surface over the step than it was at either end gets the step read inside, on CVODE's interpolant. Where the step went past the surface by more than the tolerance can blur, the run goes back to the step's start and stops inside the window.
