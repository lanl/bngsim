- **`Result.species` reported the stored `amount/V_static` of a species whose compartment a run resizes** ([#1368](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/1368)). For a concentration species in a compartment an event doubles, the SSA mean read twice the ODE value from the event on. So did ODE for an amount species (`hasOnlySubstanceUnits`) after an event. In all, five of the eight combinations were stale: event or rate rule, amount or concentration species, ODE or SSA. `as_roadrunner(["[X]"])` was right in all of them. `Result.species` now reports `amount/V_live(t)` in all eight, with the sensitivity rows of that value. **Under SSA this changes the column:** for these species it no longer holds the molecule count over `V_static`. `Result.state` keeps the stored value, and the bare `X` selector of `as_roadrunner` is the amount, as before.
