- **A fitted switch time that lands on a step of `floor(time/P)` no longer takes the step's jump** ([#944](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/944)). A step call on a clock outside every condition was placed as a solver stop and not counted among the conditions on its instant, so a fitted switch on the same instant was read with the step inside its bracket: `q*X*floor(time/3)` beside `piecewise(k*X, time >= tau, 0)` at tau = 3 returned dW/dtau = −4.2 for 0. The step is counted now and the switch is read apart from it. An event on that instant whose assignment the step does not commute with is refused by the check of #767, which now sees the step.
