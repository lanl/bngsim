- **`Result.digest(sensitivities=True)` ([#1883](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/1883)).** For each listed column, the digest now says which parameters and initial amounts move it most at the last time: up to `max_parameters` (default 5), ranked by `|p·dY/dp|` so a parameter's units do not decide the order, each with its raw derivative, `p·dY/dp`, and the scaled sensitivity `(p/y)·dY/dp` (`None` where `y` is within its tolerance of 0). A parameter at 0 or of no recorded value is listed apart, a non-finite derivative is counted, and a one-sided column carries its side.
