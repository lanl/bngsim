- **A state-dependent rate-law switch on the instant of a switch on a clock is refused** ([#946](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/946)). The jump of a switch on the state is read as the whole right-hand side a probe step before the crossing less the same a step after it. A condition on a clock that switches between the two probes was in that difference, and its jump was given the state switch's shift: `piecewise(k, time >= thr, 0)` beside `piecewise(k, S >= 0.5*thr, 0)` with S = 0.5·t returned dX/dthr = −1 for −0.5, and a fixed gate on the instant gave −0.5 to a column that is 0. With the two in one rate law the result has a kink there, and the run returned one side of it. A run with forward sensitivities now ends in an error where a switch on the state that jumps crosses within what the run's tolerances know its time to of a clock switch, fixed or fitted, and a requested parameter moves either. With no column moving either, and with the two apart, it runs as before.
