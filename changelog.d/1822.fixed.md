- **Forward sensitivity refused a model whose switch times coincide because a parameter is on its bound ([#1822](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/1822)).** With `sigma = t0 + t_delta` and `t_delta = 0`, the crossings at `t0` and `sigma` share an instant and `t0` has no parameter that moves it alone: bngsim raised `SensitivityUnsupportedError`, and a bounded optimizer that had clamped `t_delta` to 0 stopped there. One such crossing per instant is now given what is left of the instant's jump once the others are separated. Where a rate law composes the two crossings (`U()*Lambda()`), the solution has a kink in `t_delta` at 0 and its column is the derivative for `t_delta` increasing, the feasible direction at the bound: the new `bngsim.OneSidedSensitivityWarning` names the parameter and the direction, and `Result.one_sided_sensitivities` lists them. Still refused: two crossings of one instant with no parameter of their own (`t_delta = t_delta2 = 0`), two composed crossings that each have one (#951), and such a pair on two clocks, or apart by more than half the clock nudge.
