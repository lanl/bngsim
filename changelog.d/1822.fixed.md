- **Forward sensitivity refused a model whose switch times coincide because a parameter is on its bound ([#1822](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/1822)).** With `sigma = t0 + t_delta` and `t_delta = 0`, the crossings at `t0` and `sigma` share an instant, and `t0` has no parameter that moves it alone: bngsim raised `SensitivityUnsupportedError`, and a bounded optimizer that had clamped `t_delta` to 0 stopped there. Separating all but one crossing of an instant is enough. The one left over is now given the instant's merged jump, and each of the others its own jump with the left-over one's `dt*/dp` taken off. Where a rate law composes the two crossings (`U()*Lambda()`, one gate at each), the solution has a kink in `t_delta` at 0, and the `t_delta` column is the derivative for `t_delta` increasing, the feasible direction at the bound; bngsim raises the new `bngsim.OneSidedSensitivityWarning`, which names the parameter and the direction, and `Result.one_sided_sensitivities` lists them. Still refused: two or more crossings of one instant with no parameter of their own (`t_delta = t_delta2 = 0`), with a message that no longer calls the coincidence accidental, and two composed crossings that each have a parameter of their own (issue #951).
