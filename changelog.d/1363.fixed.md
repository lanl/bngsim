- **`Result.sse_gradient` documented observable data but scored it against the species** ([#1363](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/1363)). With as many observables as species, the shapes agreed, and observable data came back with an SSE of 277 for 8.5 and a gradient of the wrong sign, with no warning. `sse_gradient`, `chi2_gradient` and `neg_log_likelihood_gradient` now take `target="species"` (the default, unchanged) or `target="observables"`, which compares with `Result.observables` through `Result.sensitivities_observables`, and `observable_indices=` to select columns. Data shaped as the observables but passed against the species is refused with that hint. Also fixed, found along the way: with a per-column `sigma`, `neg_log_likelihood_gradient` counted the constant ln(2πσ²) once per column rather than once per data point, so its loss came out short (8.05 for 21.84). Its gradient was not affected.
