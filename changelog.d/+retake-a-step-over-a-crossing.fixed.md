- **A step that stepped over a rate-law switch's crossing handed back a state read on the wrong side of it, with nothing said.** CVODE finds the crossing inside the step that took it across, and the run restarts from the state CVODE's interpolant gives there. But that step read the rate law at its end, on the far side, and the interpolant carries the far side's rate over the part of the step before the crossing. A row recorded inside that step carries it too. A pulse a threshold cuts off as it falls past the state was lost: Y gained 0 of the 0.0127 the pulse gives it before the window closed, and a row just before the closing read 0 of 0.0063, at every tolerance. Such a step is now taken again from its start, up to a stop just short of the crossing, and the rows recorded inside it are recorded again, wherever what it carried exceeds a tolerance unit in some species. This applies to plain runs, not held residuals or runs with events firing or pending in the step.
