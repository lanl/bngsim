- **A codegen compile outlived a caller killed by a signal** ([#1552](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/1552)). The compiler ran in a session of its own and only Python killed it, so a caller killed at a harness's wall cap, by `SIGTERM` or `SIGKILL`, left it running for the rest of its compile (13–22 minutes for the largest models), competing for the CPU with what ran next, and leaving a library nobody installed and its `.c` source in the cache: 12.8 GB of a 13 GB cache on one machine. On Linux and macOS each compile now runs under a small supervisor that kills it and removes those files when its caller dies, however it dies.
