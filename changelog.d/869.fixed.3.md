- **A step inside another step's argument is stopped at where the outer step
  jumps, not at every jump of the inner one (follow-up to issue #869).** The
  Pokhilko and Adams circadian models (BIOMD0000000412, 445, 476) keep their
  phase with `floor(floor(time()+phase)/cyclePeriod)`, a day counter whose inner
  floor jumps every hour. Each hour was a stop and a CVODE restart where the
  rate law does not change: 4x the Jacobian evaluations on a 10-hour run. Such a
  model now stops once a cycle, and its trajectory still agrees with a
  `max_step` reference. The hourly restarts also capped the step at an hour, so
  a model whose rate is the light function itself, with no state to bound the
  step, can now step over a sharp but smooth dusk; pass `max_step` there. A
  nested step is also resolved inside a comparison, so a pulse written as
  `mod(mod(time(), 24), 12) < 1` or on a plateau of `floor(time())/24` is no
  longer stepped over. A nested step whose outer function has no known jumps,
  such as `round()`, is still stopped at every jump of the inner step.
