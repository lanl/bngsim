- **A step inside another step's argument is stopped at where the outer step
  jumps, not at every jump of the inner one (follow-up to issue #869).** The
  circadian light functions of the Pokhilko and Adams models (BIOMD0000000412,
  445, 476) keep their phase with `floor(floor(time()+phase)/cyclePeriod)`, a
  day counter whose inner floor jumps every hour. Each of those hours was a
  crossing stop and a CVODE restart where the rate law does not change, which
  cost 4x the Jacobian evaluations on a 10-hour run and 4.5x on a 10-day one.
  Such a model now stops once a cycle, with unchanged trajectories. A nested
  step is also resolved inside a comparison, so a pulse written as
  `mod(mod(time(), 24), 12) < 1` is no longer stepped over (X = 1 where 5 were
  given). A nested step whose outer function has no known jumps, such as
  `round()`, is still stopped at every jump of the inner step.
