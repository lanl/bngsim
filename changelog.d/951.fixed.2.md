- **A switch time's jump is read past its whole instant** ([#951](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/951)). Where several rate-law conditions share an instant, each one's jump was read a few ulp past its own time, with its threshold raised a hair. A neighbour a few ulp further on was then still to come: `gate(tau)*(g1 - g2)` with two fixed gates 40 and 100 ulp past tau returned dY/dtau = −1 for 0. And a parameter that both sets the threshold and scales a rate law carried the hair into the reading, a part in a million at each crossing: 0.2% of a column over the 2,000 edges of a dosing schedule. The jump is now read past the latest threshold on the instant, on every clock, and at half the hair as well and taken to none.
