- **`parameter_scan` over a parameter that seeds an initial condition dropped the dose after a run, with no warning.** Each point starts from the state at the call, and a parameter write moves a species only while it sits on its initial condition: a scan over A0 = 5 and 40 after a run started both points at the run's A = 6.07, for totals of 10 and 10 where a fresh model gives 5 and 40 (found on [#1643](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/pull/1643)). The scan keeps that start, which a pre-equilibrated protocol relies on, and now warns, naming the species it does not move, through a derived initial condition too (`A = A0*s`) and after `save_concentrations()` with no label. The warning names `Model.reset()` first, for a dose scan from the initial conditions, and an `on_point` hook, to dose from the current state.
