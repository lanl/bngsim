- **With `mask=`, `dY_ss/dp` is solved on the equations of the species the mask kept** ([#995](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/995)). A conservation law that holds a masked-out species was kept with its total, and the species it is solved for followed from the others with the masked one a constant. `S + I -> 2 I`, `I -> R` with R masked out returned dS*/dg = 0 that way, at a conditioning ratio of exactly 1 and with no warning. The masked species is now taken out of the laws by one that holds it: that law is given up, the species it was solved for becomes an unknown with its own equation, and the other laws are kept as the combinations that leave the masked species out (`A + P`, `B + P` leave `B - A`). A root of those equations that is not isolated is refused. Where the masked sink drains the law, the columns are what they were.
