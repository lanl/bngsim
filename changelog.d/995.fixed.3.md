- **With `mask=`, `dY_ss/dp` is solved on the equations of the species the mask kept** ([#995](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/995)). A conservation law that holds a masked-out species was kept with its total, and the species it is solved for followed from the others with the masked one a constant. `S + I -> 2 I`, `I -> R` with R masked out returned dS*/dg = 0 that way, at a conditioning ratio of exactly 1 and with no warning. Such a law is no longer used: the species it was solved for is an unknown with its own equation, and a root of those equations that is not isolated is refused. Where the masked sink drains the law, the columns are what they were.
