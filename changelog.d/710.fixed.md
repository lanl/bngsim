- **`steady_state()` and `steady_state_batch()` refuse a model with an event, or with a reaction rate that reads the time** ([#710](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/710)). Both solvers look for a root of `f(y)` and read the right-hand side at `t = 0` for the residual, the Newton step, the Jacobian and `∂f/∂p`; the march integrates at the true time, tests convergence at `t = 0`, and fires no event. `A -> B` at `k*(1-exp(-time()))` came back `[1, 0]` with `converged=True` after one step, where A goes to 0. A model whose event sets a production rate at `t = 2` came back at `A = 0` for `1/kd`, with `dA/dkd = 0` for `-1/kd²`. Nothing was logged. Both now raise `SimulationError`, and the message points at `run(..., steady_state=True)`, which integrates at the true time with the model's events. A `rateOf` in a rate is not a read of the time, and a function that reads the time and is in no rate does not count.
