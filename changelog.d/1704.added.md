- **`Result.digest()`: what a result's values did, for a caller that cannot look at a plot (issue #1704).** A JSON-ready dict, bounded in size, whose `str()` is a line per column: first and last values, extremes and their times, turns, whether the column settled over the last tenth of the window, and flags for a non-finite, negative or never-moving column. Turns and "settled" are judged by the steady-state code's `criterion="scaled"` tolerance at the run's own `rtol` and `atol`, so an integrator's wobble is no turn. A single stochastic run gets its values alone; a stack, statistics across runs at the last time. Past `max_columns` (20) it lists the widest ranges and counts the rest. `Result.save("run.npz")` writes the arrays, names and record with NumPy alone; a `.csv` or `.tsv` path is refused, naming `to_csv`.
