- **A concentration species in a compartment whose size moves with the run, and whose rate bngsim could not derive, was not diluted, with no error** ([#1193](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/1193)). `cv := 2 + rateOf(S3)` with S3 decaying read the `rateOf` token as a constant, so the size's rate was 0 and [X] came back as 4·e^(-t/4), the trajectory of a size that holds. A size that reads `rateOf` through another rule, or a cycle of sizes that moves (including #1185's cycle through jump conditions, which ran with NaN amounts), was left out of the dilution pass. Each is now refused at load, naming the compartment, its rule and why its rate is unknown. A size that holds still loads: the `rateOf` of a species that never moves is 0, and a cycle of constants is solved once (#621).
