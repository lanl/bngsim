- **`run(steady_state=True)` with sensitivities stopped on the state alone and handed on an unsettled `dx/dθ`** ([#1516](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/1516)). The early stop tested `||f||` only. Where a slow mode starts at its own steady state, `f` is small long before `dx/dθ` has settled in that mode: with Y at its steady state, relaxing at dy = 0.01, a run sampled every 10 time units stopped at t = 20 with `steady_state_reached` set and a last row of dX/dky = 17.96 where `dx_ss/dθ` = 100, and `carry_sensitivities=True` started the next phase from it (dXo/dky at t = 2: 16.35 for 83.33), with no warning. `run_batch`, `parameter_scan` and `bifurcate` rows took the same stop. With sensitivity columns the stop now also requires each column's `dS/dt` to pass the same test as `f`, held to `atol` (or a tighter `steady_state_tol`); the run above stops at t = 1820 with dX/dky = 99.999998. A column far larger than `atol` may never pass, and the run then goes to its end with `steady_state_reached = 0`. Runs without sensitivities stop where they did.
