A power under a half of a bend, `kb*max(thr-A,0)^0.1`, is continuous and leaves 0 with a slope that has no bound: the column came back 15% off at a relative tolerance of 1e-4 and the run did not finish at 1e-8. An equality written with ExprTk's single `=` outside any `if()`, `kb*(max(A-thr,0)=0)`, is a step no scan read: 4.41 for 2.46. A call the scan does not know, `atan2`, can be anything: its branch cut gave -1.17 for 1.30. A time course with no analytic sensitivity right-hand side is refused for each, where a column moves what it reads. A root, or any power from a half up, follows the tolerance and runs.
