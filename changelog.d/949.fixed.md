- **A crossing on the edge of a window that opens or closes as a power is
  refused for sensitivities.** Two rate-law conditions that cross on one
  instant are told apart by raising one threshold a hair with the clock past
  the instant (#375). For a window `k1*s*(1-s)^(a-1)` that reads the closing
  power a hair from its zero, and `(1e-14)^0.1` is 0.04: a jump of a few
  percent of the window's height where there is none. With a zero-order step
  on the close, or a second window closing with it, dX/dD came back 27% to
  41% off, with no warning. Such a run now stops with an error that says to
  separate the two times.
