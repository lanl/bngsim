- **A differenced Jacobian no longer reads a species far below the largest concentration as a secant** (#1002). The one-sided difference steps a species by 1.5e-8 of the larger of itself and the state's largest concentration, so beside a species at 1e8 one at 0.87 was stepped by 1.5 and `k·X²` came back with the slope `k·(2X + h)`: `steady_state(jacobian="fd", sensitivity_params=...)` returned dX*/ds 7% off beside an unrelated species at 1e7 and 44% at 1e8, and `Model.jacobian()` handed back the same matrix where it differences. The sensitivity of a function with no compiled derivative, `abs(X)*X`, was 85% off at the default settings. For such a species the row is now asked again at the species' own step, and an entry is taken from there where the row's rounding over that step is under 1e-4 of it, the row is one slope and resolved at that scale, and it is further from the first quotient than it is known to. A column is taken whole or not at all, so that its rows still add up as the right-hand side's do. Elsewhere it is bit for bit what it was: a column with an entry whose rounding is too large for the species' own step, and that of a species at exactly 0.
