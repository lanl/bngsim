- **A differenced Jacobian no longer reads a species far below the largest concentration as a secant** (#1002). The one-sided difference steps a species by 1.5e-8 of the larger of itself and the state's largest concentration, so beside a species at 1e8 one at 0.87 was stepped by 1.5 and `k·X²` came back with the slope `k·(2X + h)`: `steady_state(jacobian="fd", sensitivity_params=...)` returned dX*/ds 7% off beside an unrelated species at 1e7 and 44% at 1e8, and `Model.jacobian()` handed back the same matrix where it differences. The sensitivity of a function with no compiled derivative, `abs(X)*X`, was 85% off at the default settings. For such a species the row is now asked again at the species' own step, and an entry is taken from there where the row's rounding over that step is under 1e-4 of it, the row is smooth and resolved at that scale, and it is further from the first quotient than it is known to. Elsewhere it is bit for bit what it was: where the row's rounding is too large for the species' own step, and for a species at exactly 0.
