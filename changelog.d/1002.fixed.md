- **A differenced Jacobian no longer reads a species far below the largest concentration as a secant** (#1002). The one-sided difference steps a species by 1.5e-8 of the larger of itself and the state's largest concentration, so beside a species at 1e8 one at 0.87 was stepped by 1.5 and `k·X²` came back with the slope `k·(2X + h)`: `steady_state(jacobian="fd", sensitivity_params=...)` returned dX*/ds 7% off beside an unrelated species at 1e7 and 44% at 1e8, `Model.jacobian()` handed back the same matrix where it differences, and a species at 0 that dimerizes had a decay rate of -2.39 for -0.21. The sensitivity of a function with no compiled derivative, `abs(X)*X`, was 85% off at the default settings. A second quotient at half the step is now taken for such a species; where the two differ by more than rounding the entry is extrapolated to a step of zero, and elsewhere it is bit for bit what it was.
