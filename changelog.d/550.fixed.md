- **The emitted source of a model does not follow `PYTHONHASHSEED`** ([#550](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/550)). sympy sorts a sum's terms by their monomials over the sum's generators, which it collects in a set; two that differ only in the type of a number (`F` and the `F**1.0` that differentiating `F**2.0` leaves) tie in that sort and stay in the order of their hashes. MODEL0847869198 had two sensitivity sources that way, 8 lines of 2,099 apart, the same sums added up in a different order, and which one a process compiled followed the seed CPython drew for it. A sum's sort key is made of its ordered terms, so whatever is sorted by keys followed the seed too where it held such a sum: the terms of an enclosing sum, the factors of a product, the arguments of `min`, `max`, `and` and `or`. `bngsim._term_order` now computes the keys and takes each of those orders from them. An expression with no tie in it is printed as it was. A rate law whose saturating ratio was matched against the first symbol of a set (MODEL1006230049: rewritten in one process and not in the next) asks its symbols in the order of their names. Cached artifacts are rebuilt once (`_CODEGEN_VERSION` 39).
