- **The order a model's emitted source prints sums, products, `min`, `max`, `and` and `or` in does not follow `PYTHONHASHSEED`** ([#550](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/550)). sympy sorts a sum's terms by their monomials over the sum's generators, which it collects in a set; two that differ only in the type of a number (`F` and the `F**1.0` that differentiating `F**2.0` leaves) tie in that sort and stay in the order of their hashes. MODEL0847869198 had two sensitivity sources that way, 8 lines of 2,099 apart, the same sums added up in a different order, and which one a process compiled followed the seed CPython drew for it. A sum's sort key is made of its ordered terms, so a product's factors and the arguments of `min`, `max`, `and` and `or` followed the seed too where they held such a sum. `bngsim._term_order` computes the keys and takes each order from them; an expression with no tie is printed as it was. Still the seed's: the form sympy gives an expression as it builds one, where a condition or the argument of `sin`, `cos`, `tanh` or `abs` is a tied sum in the model's own text (`cos(u)` or `cos(-u)`). Cached artifacts are rebuilt once (`_CODEGEN_VERSION` 39).
