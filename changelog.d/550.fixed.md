- **The emitted source of a model no longer depends on `PYTHONHASHSEED`** ([#550](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/550)). Every emitter prints a sum through sympy's `as_ordered_terms`, which sorts the terms by their monomials over the sum's generators; sympy collects the generators in a set, and two that differ only in the type of a number (`F` and the `F**1.0` that differentiating `F**2.0` leaves) tie in its sort and stay in the order of their hashes. MODEL0847869198 had two sensitivity sources that way, 8 lines of 2,094 apart, the same sums added up in a different order, and which one a process compiled followed the seed CPython drew for it. The tie is now broken by what the generators are (`bngsim._term_order`). A sum with no such tie is printed as it was.
