- **The documented export pipeline packed the `.net` BNG2.pl rewrote mid-protocol, and the archive started from the wrong state with every gate level PASS ([#1683](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/1683)).** At a `simulate` with no `suffix`, BNG2.pl writes `model.net` again with the state of that moment, and `bngsim-omex pack model.net --bngl model.bngl` replayed the protocol on top of it: for `A -> 0` with an `addConcentration` of 50 before the run, the archive started at 200 for 150, and `egfr_path.bngl`'s first run at Grb2 = 100163 for 100000. Given a `.bngl`, `net_to_sbml` now checks the `.net`'s parameters and seed species against what it declares, with the actions before `generate_network` applied, and warns of and notes in `lossy` a `.net` that differs; `net_to_omex` (`bngsim-omex pack`) and `bngsim-net2sbml --sidecar` refuse it, or pack it with the warning and the note under `strict=False` / `--allow-lossy`. The interchange guide writes the network with `Model.from_bngl(..., net_out=...)`.
