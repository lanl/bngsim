- **An `EvaluationSpec` with `method="ssa"` could not name its seed, and SED-ML's random seed was dropped ([#1517](https://github.com/lanl/bngsim/issues/1517)).** The spec had no seed field, so each `evaluate()` of one SSA spec, and of its JSON round trip to a worker, drew a fresh seed, under one byte-identical `to_json()` key. `read_sedml` dropped a document's `KISAO:0000488`, and the writers wrote none, so `read_omex(...).load_protocol()` ran a seeded BNGL `simulate` on fresh seeds. A spec now has `seed`, carried through JSON and SED-ML both ways, and through `read_sedml_protocol` and `write_sedml_protocol` for a stochastic run. It is emitted only when set, so an unseeded spec keeps its bytes. An unseeded stochastic spec still draws a fresh seed, and reports it on `Result.seed`.
